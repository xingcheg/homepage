Statistics & machine learning

Xingche Guo

Assistant Professor
Department of Statistics · University of Connecticut

I develop statistical and machine learning methods for complex data from psychiatry, neuroscience, digital health, and biology.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Biostatistics at Columbia University, mentored by Prof. Yuanjia Wang.

I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from Iowa State University, advised by Prof. Dan Nettleton, Prof. Yehua Li, and Prof. Somak Dutta. I earned my B.S. in Statistics from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC).
