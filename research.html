Research

I develop statistical and machine learning methods for complex data from psychiatry, neuroscience, digital health and biology. Explore the themes below to see the work.

Decision-making dynamics and reinforcement learning

How do people learn from reward, and how does that differ from one person to the next? I build statistical and machine learning models that recover the latent cognitive states behind choices in behavioral tasks and connect them to brain activity such as EEG. The aim is a personalized description of decision-making in mental disorders.

High-dimensional functional data

When every predictor is a curve and there are many of them, which ones matter? This line builds theory and methods for regression with high-dimensional functional predictors: selecting the relevant curves, predicting with minimax guarantees, and identifying the right form of the model.

Precision medicine and digital phenotyping

Phones and wearables now record health day by day. I develop methods that turn longitudinal, biomarker and digital health data into individualized treatment strategies: estimating the sequential effects of interventions and learning biomarker-guided policies for chronic disorders.

Imaging and spatial omics

Computational tools that pull biological signal out of images and high-throughput measurements: segmenting overlapping plants in field photographs, reconstructing spatial omics data at higher resolution, and profiling metabolomic variation.
